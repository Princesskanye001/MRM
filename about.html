ABOUT MRM SPORT CAPITAL

Building value across the sports economy..

MRM Sport Capital is a Johannesburg-based, vertically integrated sports investment holding company connecting finance, technology and development to capture and create value across the sports ecosystem.

WHO WE ARE

A different model for sports investment.

MRM Sport Capital operates at the intersection of capital, innovation and sport.

Through a coordinated platform of operating businesses, strategic partnerships and diversified revenue streams, the Group identifies, structures, operates and scales opportunities across the sports ecosystem.

Our model combines investment thinking with operational execution, enabling MRM to participate across multiple stages of value creation.

OUR ORIGIN

Born in Madrid.
Built for global sport.

MRM Sport Capital was established in Madrid in 2022, following a shared vision among its founders to build an international sports investment platform connecting capital, innovation and development.

What began as a strategic concept rooted in a private equity philosophy has evolved into an operating platform with active subsidiaries, revenue-generating activities and institutional ambitions.

Today, MRM is headquartered in Johannesburg, with a growing network extending across Africa and international markets.
